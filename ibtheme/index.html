iB-theme

An IBM Carbon theme for Cursor, VS Code, GNU Emacs, Vim, Neovim, CotEditor, Apple Terminal, and iTerm2.

Dark text is #ffffff on #161616. Light text is #161616 on #ffffff. The accent is #0f62fe.

Surfaces

Cursor, VS Code, and Open VSX Extension jpfchang.ib-theme. iB-theme Dark and iB-theme Light.
GNU Emacs Load ib-theme-dark or ib-theme-light.
Vim and Neovim colorscheme ib-theme-dark or ib-theme-light.
CotEditor Import ib-theme-dark.cottheme and ib-theme-light.cottheme.
Apple Terminal and iTerm2 Dark and light profiles.
