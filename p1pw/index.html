P1PW

A warm, paper-toned light theme for Apple Terminal.

Foreground is #1A1A1A on background #F5F5F0. Bold text is #000000. The cursor is #333333. Selection is #B4D5FE.

Surfaces

Apple Terminal Import P1PW.terminal. macOS 26.4 or later.
